How working together goes

  1. We scope it

    A short call sets exactly what gets covered: a watchlist, a set of filings, one real question. Agreed before anything starts.

  2. Done for you, from public sources

    We run the research on public information: filings, company releases, news and trade press. You get the finished report, with nothing to install or learn.

  3. You get a written report

    What it found, what cuts against it, and every source it read. No buy or sell call. One section is left blank for your own judgment.

  4. NDA first, always

    Signed before we see anything specific about your watchlist or your names. No exceptions.

  5. You decide what's next

    Act on it, ignore it, or come back with a follow-up. The report is yours either way, with no obligation to continue.

What this is, and what it isn't

This is

  • One sourced quarterly report covering every portfolio company
  • A running list of likely buyers for each company, with comparable deals as disclosed
  • AI exposure and competitor moves, with what changed since last quarter
  • Every number, date and quote checked against its source and reviewed by a person before it's sent
  • An early partnership. The first clients help shape what this becomes, with direct access to the person building it

This isn't

  • Investment or M&A advice, a valuation, or a recommendation to buy or sell
  • A banker. It never makes introductions and takes no fee on any deal
  • A forecast. It reports what's public today, not what will happen next
  • Software to learn. It's delivered as a finished report
